What's satvik food?

Food cooked without onion and garlic. Plenty of families eat this way every day, or on fasts and festival days.

How do I order?

Most places here will make dishes without onion and garlic if you ask. Places marked Fully satvik don't use them at all.

Eating Jain?

Jain food also leaves out root vegetables like potato, carrot and ginger. Say "Jain" when you order, and double-check.
